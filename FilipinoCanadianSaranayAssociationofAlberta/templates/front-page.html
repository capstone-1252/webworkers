ABOUT US

Over the past 40 years, Saranay has brought benefits to Filipinos and their families, including youth, adult, and senior population through activities and events!

SARANAY is a Filipino maxim, which means "to help and to serve others".

Our association welcomes all who share values of caring, and compassion .

Our Mission is to help and to serve Filipinos in Edmonton
